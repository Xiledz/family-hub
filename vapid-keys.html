Leave this page open until you've pasted everything. Refreshing generates a different pair — and if the public key in config.js doesn't match the private key in Supabase, notifications fail silently.

What these actually are

  1. A matched pair. Apple and Google won't deliver a notification unless it's signed by a key they can verify came from your app. The private key does the signing on Supabase's server; the public key is what your family's phones check it against.
  2. Not a login. Nobody can read your calendar with these. The worst case if the private key leaked is that someone could send notifications to devices already subscribed to your hub.
  3. Generate once. If you ever replace them, everyone has to re-enable notifications, because their existing subscriptions were tied to the old public key.
