Privacy Policy

Family Hub · Last updated 20 August 2026

Family Hub is a private calendar used by one household. It is not a commercial service, it has no customers, and it is not open to public sign-up. This policy describes what it stores and how text messaging is handled.

What we collect

  • First names of household members
  • Calendar events created by household members
  • Mobile phone numbers of household members, where a member has chosen to receive reminders by text message
  • Web push subscription identifiers issued by the member's own browser

Mobile information is never shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party. Phone numbers are used only to deliver calendar reminders to the household member who provided the number, and to identify that member when they text an event into the calendar.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on how many calendar events a household member has chosen to be reminded about. In typical use this is fewer than ten messages per week per person.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Family Hub does not charge for messages; your mobile carrier's standard rates apply.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for assistance. A member's number can also be removed from the household calendar at any time on request.

Where the data lives

Calendar data is stored in a private Supabase (PostgreSQL) database controlled by the account holder. The web application is served as static files from GitHub Pages. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used, and no data is sold, rented, or transferred to anyone.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the account holder deletes it. Any household member may ask the account holder to delete their information, and it will be removed.

Children

This calendar includes the account holder's own minor children, added by their parent. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from children outside the household.
