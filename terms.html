Family Hub · Last updated 20 August 2026

What this is

Family Hub is a private shared calendar for a single household. Access requires a household passcode issued by the account holder. It is not offered to the public and there is no public registration.

Text messaging program

Household members may choose to receive calendar reminders by text message and to add calendar events by texting the household number.

  • Program name: Family Hub calendar reminders
  • Who receives messages: only members of the account holder's immediate household who have given their mobile number directly to the account holder
  • Message frequency: varies with the number of calendar events a member has asked to be reminded about; typically fewer than ten messages per week
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply. There is no charge from Family Hub

How members opt in

Household members give their mobile number verbally, in person, to the account holder, who enters it into the private calendar. There is no web form and no public sign-up. Members are told at that time that they will receive calendar reminders and may stop at any time.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or contact the address below.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed and should not be relied on for time-critical or emergency purposes.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Mobile information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Acceptable use

The calendar is for household scheduling. Members must not use it to send unlawful, harassing, or commercial content.
