# Family Calendar — setting it up on your phone

Takes about a minute. You don't need to download anything from the App Store.

## iPhone / iPad

1. Open this link **in Safari** (it has to be Safari, not Chrome):

   **`<PASTE YOUR LINK HERE>`**

2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen (the square with the
   arrow pointing up).

3. Scroll down the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

4. Close Safari. You now have a **Family** icon on your home screen — open it
   from there from now on.

5. Enter the family code: **`<PASTE THE CODE HERE>`**

6. Tap your name.

7. When it asks about reminders, tap **Turn on**, then **Allow**.

That's it.

> **Why the Home Screen step matters:** Apple only lets notifications work from
> the icon, not from a Safari tab. If you skip it, everything works except the
> reminders.

## Android

Open the link in Chrome, tap the **⋮** menu, tap **Install app** or **Add to
Home screen**. Then same as steps 5–7 above.

## Computer

Just open the link and bookmark it. Everything works except notifications.

## Using it

**Adding something is one line.** Type it the way you'd say it:

```
Soccer practice Thursday 5:30 Noah
Dentist tomorrow 9am Mia
Church picnic Saturday all day
Date night Friday 7pm remind 1 hour before
```

It shows you what it understood — check it and tap **Add it**. If it got
something wrong, tap **Edit** instead and fix it.

**Don't want to type?** Tap the microphone key on your iPhone keyboard and just
say the whole thing out loud.

**Reminders.** Every event can alert you ahead of time — 10 minutes, 30 minutes,
an hour, a day, whatever you pick. So if something starts at noon and you need to
leave by 11:30, set it to 30 minutes and your phone buzzes at 11:30.

You can also set your own default so you never have to think about it — if you
always want 30 minutes' warning, it just does that every time.

**Everyone sees the same calendar,** and it updates on everybody's phone the
moment someone adds something.
