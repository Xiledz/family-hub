# Family Hub — the one page

Calendar, shopping list, chores and dinner, on everyone's phone. Takes two
minutes to set up. Nothing to download from the App Store.

## 1. Put it on your phone (iPhone)

1. Open this link **in Safari** (not Chrome — Apple only allows the next step from Safari):

   **https://xiledz.github.io/family-hub/**

2. Enter the family code: **`KELLEY2009`** — then tap your name.

3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow, bottom of the screen).

4. Scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

5. Close Safari. Open the new **Family Hub** icon on your home screen.

6. **It will ask for the code again. That's normal** — the icon is its own
   little app and doesn't share Safari's memory. Type it once more, tap your
   name again, and you're done for good.

7. When it asks about reminders, tap **Turn on**, then **Allow**.

> The home-screen icon matters: Apple only lets alerts through from the icon,
> never from a Safari tab. If alerts aren't arriving, check you opened it from
> the icon.

**Android:** open the link in Chrome → **⋮** → **Add to Home screen**. Same code, same steps.
**Computer:** just open the link and bookmark it (no alerts on a computer).

## 2. Using the app

- **Type things the way you'd say them.** Each tab has its own box at the top.
- **Calendar** — everyone's, live. Box at the top: `Soccer Thursday 5:30 Bryce`,
  `Dentist tomorrow 9am Addie`. Tap an event to change it or tick it off.
  **A whole season at once:** copy the schedule (one date per line, with a
  first line like `Orchestra — Addie, Jess driving`) and paste it into that
  box — you get one list to check and one **Add** button. Same by text.
- **Shopping** — one shared list. Box: `milk, eggs, paper towels`. Tick things
  as you put them in the cart, then **Done shopping** when you're done.
- **To-do** — a list per person, dragged into whatever order you want. Box:
  `Bryce take out the trash every Tuesday`. Tap the circle when it's done. A
  chore that repeats comes back on its own; a missed one shows **Missed**.
- **Meals** — plan dinner for the week; the reminders (take the meat out,
  start cooking) go to whoever is cooking.

## 4. The kids

Addie and Bryce don't have phones, so **their reminders come to Jess** — a
text like *"For Bryce: Take out the trash — Due today. Reply DID when done."*
Reply **DID** and it's ticked off on his list. Their chores also show up in
the morning summary, and on their own lists in the app on any phone in the
house — tap their name at the top of the To-do tab.

## 5. Grandparents (or a sitter)

They don't need the app. Open **Settings** (tap your name at the top) →
**Share the calendar** → **Copy the link**, and text it to them. On their
iPhone: **Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add
Subscribed Calendar**, paste the link. The family calendar then shows up
inside their own Calendar app, read-only, and stays current on its own.

## 6. Every morning

Around 6:30 you'll get one message: your day, what's for dinner and who's
cooking, and anything on your list that's due. That's the whole system —
if you read that one message, you're caught up.
